What does it mean to add games together?
========================================

fractions.png

Proof:

2p + 1
------  =  2p+1 copies of 1/2^(n+1) = 2p+1 copies of {0|1/2^n}
2^(n+1)

If Left goes, they must change a 1/2^(n+1) into a 0.

If Right goes, they must change a 1/2^(n+1) into a 1/2^n.
