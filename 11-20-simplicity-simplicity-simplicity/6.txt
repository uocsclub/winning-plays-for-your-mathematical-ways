Simplest Forms for Numbers
==========================

numbertree.png

Each fork of the tree is a number whose best options are the nearest numbers left and right of it that are higher up.

Canonical/simplest forms: simplest.png

For example, 1½ = {1|2}, 13/16 = {¾|⅞}, 79 = {78| }, -53 = { |-52},
47/64 = {23/32|24/32} = {23/32|¾}

The simpler a number is, the nearer it is to the root/top of the tree!
