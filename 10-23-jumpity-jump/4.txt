What is a jump worth?
=====================

Recall: before, we said that a jump was worth half a move. But in fact:

jumpworth.png

- A jump for Blue is +1/2 if it's unavoidable when Blue starts (ie both players are the same distance from one square). (Red could avoid it by losing further)

- A jump for Blue is +0 if it's not unavoidable when Blue starts and Blue wins the position

- A jump for Blue is +1 if it's not unavoidable when Blue starts and Red wins the position (also moves a Red win to a zero game)
