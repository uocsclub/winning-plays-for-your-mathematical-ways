What is a game?
===============

1. two players (can be called Left and Right)
2. several *positions*, usually a *starting position*
3. *rules* specify valid *moves* from a given position to its *options*
4. Left and Right move alternately
5. *normal play* convention: a player unable to play *loses*
6. play will always eventually come to an end: the *ending condition*
7. *complete information*
8. there are no *chance moves*

Consider: hackenbush toads-and-frogs ski-jumps tic-tac-toe chess snakes-and-ladders backgammon battleships rock-paper-scissors monopoly solitaire life poker bridge tennis dots-and-boxes go
