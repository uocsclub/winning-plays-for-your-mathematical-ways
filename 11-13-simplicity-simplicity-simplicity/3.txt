When is a move good?
====================

"good": it will win for you
"bad": it will lose for you

but not all good moves are equally good! not all bad moves are equally bad!

if you're losing, may prefer a move that complicates the game for your opponent (enough rope principle)

or maybe you even prefer a bad move to a good one!
