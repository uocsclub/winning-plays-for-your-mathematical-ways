The value of a tree
===================

question2.png

		Answer:
		9 1/2, of course!

		Red's best move leads to a position of value 10, and Blue's best move leads to a position of value 9. So we write {9|10} = 9 1/2 ("9 slash 10 equals 9 1/2").

		More generally, we always have the equation {n|n+1} = n + 1/2.

		Also, {n| } = n + 1

		{ | } = 0.
