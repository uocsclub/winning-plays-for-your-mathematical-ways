Ski-Jumps for Beginners
=======================

More generally, if
- Left has a single skiier on the board with a spaces (and hence a+1 moves) before him
- Right has a single skiier on the board with b spaces before /him/
- One of the two can jump over the other

What will the value be?

    Answer
    If Left is above: {(a+1)-(b+1)|(a+1)-b} = {a-b|a-b+1} = a-b+1/2
    If Right is above: {a-(b+1)|(a+1)-(b+1)} = {a-b-1|a-b} = a-b-1/2

		So an imminent jump is worth about half a move to the player who can make it.
