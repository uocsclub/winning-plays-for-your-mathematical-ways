Ski-Jumps for Beginners
=======================

- Left's skiiers move a square or more Eastward
- Right's skiiers move a square or more Westward
- If a skiier is immediately above an opposing one, they may jump them
- The skiier jumped over is so humiliated that he is demoted from being a *jumper* to being a *slipper*
- If you cannot move, you lose

What are the values of these positions? skijumps1.png

		Answers:
		(a) 5 - 3 = 2 for Left
		(b) 5 + 2 - 8 for Left, that is, 1 for Right
    (c) if Left goes first, he should jump, for a value of 4 - 2 = 2
		    if Right goes first, the value is 4 - 1 = 3.
		    So this position has value {2|3} = 2 1/2.
